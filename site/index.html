Caveats.
  • arXiv is a self-selected preprint corpus and skews toward CS/physics.
  • This extraction from LaTeX source likely under-estimates actual accelerator prevalence, as extraction errors or lack of in-paper reporting may introduce a downward bias: trends are more meaningful than actual quantities.
  • Reported compute/memory figures are derived by matching paper-reported hardware to its closest specification and should be understood as an upper bound estimate on compute actually used by the work.
  • Device-count and per-device quantities assume one device when a paper reports accelerator use without specifying an exact number of devices used.
